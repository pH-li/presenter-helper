Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

  • You can use Presenter Helper without an account. Your notes then stay in your browser and never reach us.
  • If you sign in, we store your email address and your presentations so they sync across your devices.
  • We don’t show ads, sell your data, use analytics or tracking, or use your notes to train AI.
  • You can export your presentations and delete your account at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

Presenter Helper (“we”, “us”) is a small independent web app for speakers, available at presentassist.netlify.app. You can reach us at lyrwrites@gmail.com.

What we collect

If you don’t sign in

Your presentations are saved only in your browser’s local storage on your device. We don’t receive them.

If you sign in

  • Email address: used to send you sign-in links and to identify your account.
  • Your presentations: titles, speaking notes, slide order, target lengths, slide times and display settings such as text size.
  • Account records: when your account was created and when you last signed in, kept by our authentication provider.

Technical data

Like almost every website, the services that host and run Presenter Helper automatically receive basic technical information when your browser connects to them. That includes your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, and it’s used to deliver the site and to keep it secure.

Stored on your device

The app uses your browser’s local storage to keep a copy of your presentations, so you can present offline. If you sign in, it also keeps your sign-in session there. This storage is needed for the app to work. We don’t use cookies or local storage for advertising or tracking, so there’s no cookie banner.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To store your presentations and sync them between your devices.
  • To send sign-in emails. We don’t send marketing email.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To respond when you contact us.

If you’re in the EU or UK, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: providing the service you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests: security and abuse prevention.

Who handles your data

We use these service providers to run Presenter Helper. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and sign-in emails. Your account and presentations are stored in Supabase’s US East (Ohio) region.
  • Netlify: hosts the website and receives standard request logs.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the Supabase code library to your browser, and receives standard request information.

We don’t sell or share your personal information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

If you use Presenter Helper from outside the United States, your data will be transferred to and stored in the United States.

Who can see your presentations

Your presentations are private to your account. Other users can’t see them: the database only allows each signed-in user to read and change their own presentations.

As the operator, we can technically access the database. We only look at stored data when it’s needed to help with something you asked us about, to investigate abuse or a security issue, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and presentations until you delete them. Deleting a presentation removes it from our database, and deleting your account removes your email address and all your presentations immediately. Copies may remain in our providers’ backups for a short period before those backups are overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Get a copy: use Export backup on the home page to download all your presentations as a file.
  • Correct: edit your presentations at any time.
  • Delete: delete individual presentations, or your whole account under Account → Delete account.
  • Anything else: email lyrwrites@gmail.com, for example to ask what data we hold about you. We’ll reply within 30 days.

If you’re in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. If you’re in California, note that we don’t sell or “share” personal information as California law defines those terms.

Children

Presenter Helper isn’t intended for children under 13. You must be at least 13, or older if your country requires it, to create an account. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

We protect your data in three ways:

  • Every connection to the site and the database is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The database only lets each user reach their own presentations.
  • Our secret database keys are never sent to your browser.

No system is perfectly secure, so please don’t store highly sensitive information such as passwords or medical records in your notes.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we’ll also point them out in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: lyrwrites@gmail.com
